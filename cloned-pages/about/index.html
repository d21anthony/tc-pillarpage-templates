We put your health first

Watch our videos

TrueCare is one of the most trusted health care providers in San Diego and Riverside Counties. Our friendly staff and compassionate physicians specialize in providing quality health care and wellness services to underserved communities so people can get the care they need. Our goal is to make health care available to everyone in the communities we serve, regardless of income or insurance status.

Over 50 Years of Heartfelt Care

In 1971 a small group of dedicated health professionals identified a need to provide comprehensive and affordable quality health care to our diverse and underserved communities. They launched North County Health Services (now known as TrueCare) to provide individuals and families with much-needed health care. From our first health care clinic opening in 1974 we have steadily grown into a network of thriving health centers. Half a century later, we’re still growing!

Michelle D. Gonzalez, MPA

President and CEO

Michelle Gonzalez’ roots at TrueCare™ run deep. Starting out as a Program Coordinator nearly 20 years ago, she went on to hold several senior management positions, including Chief Strategy and Development Officer and VP of Strategic Initiatives, before being appointed to President and CEO in April of 2020. Her broad responsibilities instilled a deep understanding of the unique challenges facing nonprofit health care organizations and the communities they serve.

Michelle’s time with TrueCare also fostered a deep and abiding commitment to the organization’s mission of providing affordable, quality care to anyone in need. Under her leadership, TrueCare has expanded access to its services while improving the patient care experience. This includes establishing the patient call center, opening new clinic locations, and transitioning to the best in class, OCHIN-Epic (Electronic Health Records technology). Michelle also spearheaded a comprehensive re-brand and name change to TrueCare (formerly North County Health Services) to elevate brand awareness.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, under Michelle’s leadership, TrueCare launched a number of strategic initiatives to ensure care providers and staff had the resources to protect themselves while providing quality care. These included deploying strict safety protocols, rapid deployment of telehealth services, and the expansion of COVID-19 testing and vaccination sites at TrueCare locations.

To attract highly skilled health care professionals who share her passion for TrueCare’s mission, Michelle works hard to build a culture of caring and compassion. Her leadership style is driven by clear communication to all employees, rewarding excellence, inspiring greatness and instilling shared values that celebrate individuality. Her ability to foster crisp alignment between the Board of Directors, Executive Team and Staff Leadership keeps TrueCare on the cutting-edge of health care innovation.

Michelle holds a Master’s Degree in Public Administration, as well as a certificate in the Not-For-Profit Management Development Program through the Harvard Business School, San Diego Chapter, and a Green Belt Certification in Lean Six Sigma. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with her husband and two young children; keeping them closely connected to their roots with family from Mexico and Guam.

Our Health Partners

NCQA Quality Recognition

TrueCare is proud to be a fully-accredited, Patient-Centered Medical Home. As an organization, we are recognized through the National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) as a Patient-Centered Medical Home. This verifies our ability to provide the highest quality care and ensures a dedicated team of health professionals will deliver the medical care you need.

CaliforniaHealth+

TrueCare is part of a network of community health centers that serve everyone in our communities, regardless of ability to pay. We are committed to taking care of you and your family with quality medical care, including prenatal, dental, lab and x-ray services, transportation and more. TrueCare is proud to serve as a trusted friend and partner in your lifelong health care journey.

Covered California

TrueCare was one of the first health centers in North County to become a Certified Enrollment Entity through Covered CA. Our bilingual staff is always ready to help you find a Covered CA or Medi-Cal plan that works for you and your budget. We also help you fill out and submit the paperwork, and even help you make your first appointment as an insured person or family.

Human Rights Campaign Foundation

TrueCare is proud to be recognized as one of only three community health centers in California, and the only one in North County San Diego and Riverside, designated as a High Performer on the 2024 Healthcare Equality Index (HEI). This is a national LGBTQ+ benchmarking tool that evaluates health care facilities’ policies and practices related to the integration of LGBTQ+ patients, visitors, and employees.
